Questions

Rates, passes and safety

Rates include VAT and are fictional.

0 / 7

01How is the hourly fee calculated?

The first hour is ₺60, each further hour ₺30; the cap for 24 hours is ₺300. Rates include VAT and are fictional.

02What if I lose my ticket?

The daily cap is charged; you leave after a registration and ID check.

03What does a monthly pass cover?

24/7 entry and exit at your chosen branch and barrier passage without stopping by plate recognition; ₺2,400 a month.

04Can I use my pass at another branch?

Yes; up to 3 daytime hours at the other two branches at no extra charge, then the hourly rate.

05Can tall vehicles get in?

Basement levels have a 2.10 m clearance; minibuses and cars with roof boxes use the ground level.

06Can I charge my electric car?

Konak branch has 12 charging points on the roof level; charging is billed separately, parking at the normal rate.

07Are there cameras and staff on the levels?

Every level is recorded on camera and the night attendant does rounds; the insurance policy summary is shown at the pass desk (fictional).
